Dog Encyclopedia

Here are three great breeds. Each card shows a simple drawing plus easy notes about its size, personality, and everyday care.

Beagle

  • Size: Small. About 33–41 cm tall and 9–11 kg.
  • Personality: Happy, curious, and friendly. Loves to sniff and follow scents.
  • Everyday care: Needs daily walks and play. Brush once a week. Keep it on a lead—its nose loves to wander!

Golden Retriever

  • Size: Large. About 51–61 cm tall and 25–34 kg.
  • Personality: Gentle, clever, and eager to please. Great with families and children.
  • Everyday care: Needs lots of exercise and brushing two or three times a week. Loves games like fetch.

Poodle

  • Size: Varies. Toy is tiny (under 28 cm); Standard is large (over 45 cm).
  • Personality: Smart, proud, and lively. Learns tricks quickly and loves attention.
  • Everyday care: Needs regular grooming and clipping. Daily play keeps its busy mind happy.
